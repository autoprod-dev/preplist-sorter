Privacy

Prepline, the prep list sorter by Autoprod. Short version: your recipes stay with you.

Your recipes and lists stay on your device

Prepline runs in your web browser. The menus, PDFs and spreadsheets you open, and the lists it makes, are kept in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up and no login. We don't use analytics, tracking cookies or advertising.

Feedback and the survey are optional

Feedback, Report a problem and the 1-minute survey only reach Autoprod (autoprod.help@gmail.com) when you choose to send them. Feedback and Report a problem open your own email app with the text ready (or let you copy it), so you see exactly what is sent. The survey is a separate page you open yourself.

The survey email field is optional

The survey asks for an email only if you would like early access to new features. You can leave it blank and your answers stay anonymous. If you add it, it is only used for early access and never shared.

Questions

Email autoprod.help@gmail.com.
