Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Prepline is made by Autoprod. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Prepline and Prepline Pro.

The short version

  • Your recipes and lists stay on your device. Menus, PDFs, spreadsheets, prep lists and recipes are opened and processed in your own browser. We never receive them. There is no account or password.
  • Your name and role. When you first open Prepline we ask your name and role. These, with the date and time, are saved to a private Google Sheet run by Autoprod so we can see who's trying it. Not used for marketing, not shared or sold.
  • Pro licence checks send only your licence key (plus a technical activation ID and a generic device label like "Prepline - Windows Chrome - 2026-10-08") to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, to confirm it's valid. This happens when you activate Pro and then at most about once a week.
  • Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. We never see your card details.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.

1. What stays on your device

Prepline saves some things in your browser's local storage so they're there next time: your recipe database, current list, templates, stations and kitchens, your preferences and, if you bought Pro, your licence key, activation ID and when it was last checked. This data stays on that device. You can delete it by clearing your browser's site data (export a backup first).

2. Your name and role (welcome box)

The first time you open Prepline online it asks your name and role. These, with the date and time, are sent to a private Google Sheet (Google Workspace, via Google Apps Script) that only Autoprod can see. Your recipes, menus and lists are never sent with it. To have your entry removed, email autoprod.help@gmail.com.

3. What Lemon Squeezy receives

When you buy: Lemon Squeezy collects what it needs to sell to you, such as your name, email, billing country/address and payment details, and issues your receipt and licence key. Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and handles payments, GST/sales tax, refund processing and receipts. Its privacy policy applies: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

When the App checks your key: the licence key, activation (instance) ID and device label, sent from your browser directly to api.lemonsqueezy.com. Nothing about your files is included. Lemon Squeezy (and your browser) will also see your IP address, as with any web request.

4. What we (Autoprod) receive

From Lemon Squeezy's dashboard we can see order details for Pro purchases: name, email, country, product, price, and the licence key's activations (with their device labels). We use this only to provide Pro, handle support and refunds, meet tax and record-keeping obligations, and (only if you opted in at checkout) send product updates.

Feedback, problem reports and the survey are optional. Feedback and Report a problem open your own email app with the text ready (or let you copy it), so you see exactly what is sent; they reach us only if you send them. The 1-minute survey is a separate page you open yourself; its email field is optional and only used for early access to new features. Please don't send us your files unless you want us to look at them to fix a problem; we delete them once the issue is resolved.

5. Other services the page loads

To work, the website loads its spreadsheet and PDF libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, those services see your IP address and browser details. None of your files or lists are sent to them. The offline Windows version loads nothing from the internet.

6. Where data is held

Lemon Squeezy is based in the United States and may store purchase data overseas. Support email is handled with Gmail and the welcome-box sheet with Google Sheets; Google may store this data outside Australia.

7. How long we keep it

Order records: as long as tax law requires (generally 5 years in Australia). Support emails: up to 2 years. Welcome-box entries: until you ask us to remove them or we no longer need them. Data on your device: until you delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (except records we must keep by law). Email autoprod.help@gmail.com. If you're not happy with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

9. Children

Prepline is a general tool and isn't directed at children under 13. Pro purchases require the buyer to be able to enter a contract.

10. Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes and note significant changes on the website.
